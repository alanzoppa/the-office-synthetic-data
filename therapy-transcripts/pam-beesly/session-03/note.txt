Assessment

Session 3 completes the assessment phase. The drawing homework was a breakthrough — not because the art is exceptional (it's a pencil sketch) but because the client returned to a self-initiated creative act for the first time since art school. The key insight: the problem isn't her ability; it's her internal judge. When the judge took a break, she could draw.

The mother-voice identification is the origin story. Unlike Michael's father (who left), Pam's origin is a relationship that was present but critical — a mother who shaped her daughter's wants through soft, persistent disapproval disguised as support. The "not good enough" voice is internalized maternal criticism.

The treatment plan is accepted. The client's response ("nobody's ever looked at me and said here's what I see") confirms that being seen — without evaluation, without the mother's voice — is the core therapeutic experience she needs.
