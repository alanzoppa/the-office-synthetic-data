client: Like being between two rooms. I'm in the Roy room and I can see the other room — the room where I'm not with Roy — and the door is open. And I'm standing in the doorway. Not moving. And I'm standing in the door.
clinician: And the stone is the knowing.
client: The stone is the knowing. And the knowing doesn't change regardless of which room I'm in. The knowing is true in both rooms. I don't want Roy in the Roy room and I don't want Roy in the other room. The knowing doesn't care where I'm standing. It's just true.
