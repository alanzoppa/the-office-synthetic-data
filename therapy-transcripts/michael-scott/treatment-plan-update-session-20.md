## Progress Summary

Client has completed 20 sessions of weekly integrative therapy (MI-informed CBT with attachment/psychodynamic awareness). Significant progress has been achieved across all four original treatment goals. This update reflects the current status and redirects focus for the remaining 5 sessions (21-25) before planned termination.

## Goal Status

### Goal 1: Increase Awareness of Performance Pattern — ACHIEVED
Client has progressed from no awareness (session 1) → retrospective awareness (sessions 3-6) → real-time awareness (sessions 7-14) → metacognitive awareness (sessions 15-20). Client can now observe the performance running even in contexts where he believed it was absent, including within the therapeutic relationship. No further active work required; client maintains awareness informally.

### Goal 2: Develop Ability to Tolerate Being Present Without Performing — SUBSTANTIALLY ACHIEVED
Client completed five behavioral experiments (Pam x3, Oscar x1, Jim x1), all successful. Client can sit in unscripted silence (20 seconds voluntarily in session 19). Client has generalized boundary-setting to live interactions (Dwight, Jan, clinician). Sitting practice for core affective state ("nobody's coming") established and effective — feeling intensity has decreased from ~40 seconds of acute distress to brief, tolerable waves. No further structured experiments needed; client maintains practice independently.

### Goal 3: Begin Identifying Personal Needs Independent of Others — ACTIVE FOCUS
Client has identified two genuine (non-performed) traits: curiosity and warmth. Client has identified two non-performed activities: private mix CD creation ("the songs do the feeling for me") and solo night walks ("just a guy walking"). Client cannot yet articulate a coherent identity outside of occupational role. The condo is experienced as "a waiting room between performances." This goal is the primary focus for sessions 21-25.

### Goal 4: Explore Early Attachment Experiences — UNDERSTANDING ACHIEVED, CHANGE ONGOING
Client accessed and processed foundational attachment memories (father leaving, sitting by the door, stepfather's disengagement, mother's responsive engagement to performance). Connected childhood patterns to current relationships (Jan as replay of waiting; Toby as replay of Jeff's disengagement; Dundies as manufactured family). Client holds awareness that "staying with Jan may be self-abandonment" but has not taken action. This is appropriate — the understanding phase is complete; action will follow on the client's timeline.
