Subjective

Client returned after the rupture. He described sitting in the car, thinking: "If I don't go in, I'm hovering. And hovering is the thing I'm mad at you for saying I do. So not going in is proving your point." He caught his own meta-level avoidance ("overthinking the meta-level to avoid the actual level") and came in.

The therapist apologized: "The interpretation was accurate but the timing was wrong. You were vulnerable after the design program disclosure, and I pushed on the chair material too fast." The therapist clarified: "I wasn't saying you ARE your father. I was saying the hover has become familiar, and familiarity can become a trap."

Client accepted the repair and articulated the key insight: "The witness makes the hovering a choice. Because if I know I'm hovering and I hover anyway, it's not a reflex. It's a decision. And I can't pretend it's automatic anymore." He reported the week was heavy — "knowing I'm choosing to hover, choosing Karen, choosing not to tell Pam. All of it is chosen. And chosen feels heavier than reflex."

He confirmed the repair: "You pushed, I ran. But I came back. That's the new part. The old Jim would have quit therapy and told Pam 'I tried.' But I came back. The repair is: I came back."

He acknowledged the interpretation was correct: "The hovering is the loss. You said that and I hated it and it's true."

Homework: look at design programs again, but don't shut down. Sit with the pull. Stay on the ground with the wanting without escaping.
