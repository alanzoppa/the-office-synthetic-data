Subjective

Client wrote the five coping phrases on a sticky note attached to the Pratt certificate browser tab. Used "being present is not being trapped" during a boring meeting — stayed on the ground for the entire meeting. "Being present for boring is the real test."

Homework: say the honest sentence to Michael this week. See what happens.
