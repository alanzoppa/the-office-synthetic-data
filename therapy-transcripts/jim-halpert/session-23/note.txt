Session Type: Individual Therapy (Internalization & Pam Step Processing)

Subjective

Client took the Pam step. Friday after work: "Pam, I'm not over it." He reported staying on the ground for the entire conversation — no hovering, no joke, no shrug. Pam's response: "Jim, I'm with Roy." Jim: "I know. I'm not asking you to do anything. I'm just telling you." Pam: "I need to think about this." Jim: "Take all the time you need. I'll be here."

Client reported: "It hurt. The not-knowing hurts. But the hurt is alive. And I'd rather be alive and hurting than numb and safe."

The session co-created five coping phrases:
1. "Not caring is not safety. Not caring is its own trap."
2. "Choosing is not the same as being stuck."
3. "Trying and failing is not the same as not trying."
4. "I am not my father. His chair is not my chair."
5. "Being present is not being trapped."

Client noted: "I'm just landing now. The 'learning to' part is falling away. I'm just landing."

Assessment

The Pam step is the Jim equivalent of Michael's telling Pam she made a difference (24) and Pam's telling her mother "it matters to me" (24). All three characters complete the final vulnerability test — the honest interpersonal action with the person who matters most.

Jim's outcome is different from Michael's and Pam's: Jim doesn't get a clear positive response. Pam says "I need to think about this." The outcome is uncertain. This is clinically significant — Jim's arc ends with ambiguity, not resolution. He did the brave thing and the result is not-knowing. His ability to sit with not-knowing is the ultimate proof that the therapy worked: the hover was about not-knowing; now he can be on the ground with not-knowing.

The "I'm just landing now" formulation is the Jim equivalent of Michael's "just showing up is enough" and Pam's "honest is better than nice." Each character's final reframe reflects their arc: Michael (performance → presence), Pam (suppression → honesty), Jim (avoidance → landing).
