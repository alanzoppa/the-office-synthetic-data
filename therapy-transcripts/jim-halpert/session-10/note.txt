Subjective

Client called his father — four minutes of "how's the job" / "fine" / "good." He reported being on the ground for all four minutes. Key insight: "The fear made it huge. The reality was tiny. And tiny is manageable." The chair was huge; the phone call was tiny. He was afraid of the chair, not the phone call.

Design project success is settling: "It's the first thing I've done in years where I genuinely tried and the trying produced a result. What if the hovering was the only thing actually stopping me?"

New formulation: "small landings." Instead of big escapes (Stamford) or big deflections (jokes), small honest actions: four minutes on the phone, one design project, one honest sentence. "Small. On the ground. Real."

Homework: one honest sentence with Karen — not a joke, something real. Client noted: "She knows funny Jim. She doesn't know real Jim."
